FAQ

Straight answers

Including what this is not.

Is this legal advice?

No, and we won't pretend it is. PolicyPack generates documents from your answers using clause templates for your jurisdiction. It's a real, complete starting point for typical founder needs — but no lawyer reviews your output, and for high-stakes or unusual situations you should talk to one.

How is this different from a subscription generator?

Subscription services bundle things we don't: ongoing compliance updates and consent-management tooling. What we offer is different — a one-time, downloadable pack you own outright. If you want managed, always-updated compliance, a subscription is the right buy. If you want solid documents you keep, that's us.

Can I regenerate or change my answers later?

Yes. Your draft autosaves, and you can change any answer and regenerate the documents whenever you like before you download. Once downloaded, the files are yours to edit like any HTML or text file.
