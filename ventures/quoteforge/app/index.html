FAQ

Straight answers

No fine print, no sales rep required.

Will the price match what I charge on-site?

It's a range, by design — low to high, rounded to the nearest 10, based on the modifiers you define. You set the questions that matter for your work, so the range reflects your real pricing logic, and you confirm the final number when you see the job.

Do customers need an account or app?

No. They open your link in any phone browser, pick a service, answer your questions, see the range, and leave their contact info. Nothing to install on either end.

Can I cancel in the off-season?

Yes — it's month-to-month. Cancel through Stripe in the off-season, come back in spring. Your lead history exports to CSV before you go, so nothing is trapped inside.
