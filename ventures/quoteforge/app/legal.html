The honest soft gate

QuoteForge costs $29/month (see pricing). Online checkout via Stripe Payment Links is still being wired up — so right now nothing in this app is locked, gated, or faked. You get the full setup flow, the shareable customer link, and the lead dashboard today. When checkout goes live, paying customers keep everything working exactly as-is; that's the whole point of a soft gate.

Your data stays in your browser

QuoteForge v1 has no backend and no accounts. Your business setup and your leads live in this browser's localStorage — the same device you set them up on. That means:

  • Nothing is uploaded to our servers, because there are no servers.
  • Leads captured on a customer's phone reach you via the mailto email bridge in the quote flow (their email app, prefilled) — plus you can export everything to CSV any time.
  • Clearing your browser's site data erases your setup and leads. Export CSV regularly; it's your backup.
  • Leads do not sync across your devices in v1.

How the price ranges work

Every range is computed from the pricing logic you define: base price plus your modifier questions. Low = base + the cheapest possible combination of answers; high = base + the most expensive combination; both rounded to the nearest 10. The customer's answers also produce a single "most similar jobs land around" estimate, marked on the range bar. Ranges are estimates, not binding quotes — you confirm the final price when you see the job.

v2 notes — cut from v1, not forgotten

These were deliberately left out of v1 so the core could ship solid. Each is on the v2 roadmap:

  • Real SMS sending. v1 uses your phone's tel: and sms-style links — nothing sends automatically. v2: opt-in SMS notifications via a messaging provider.
  • In-flow payments. v1 links out for payment only. v2: optional deposit collection inside the customer flow via Stripe.
  • Multi-user accounts. v1 is one workspace per browser — no logins, no team seats. v2: accounts with team access and roles.
  • Calendar booking. v1 ends at the quote + lead. v2: let customers pick an on-site visit slot right after their quote.
  • Cross-device lead sync. v1 keeps leads on the device that captured them, bridged by email. v2: cloud sync across your phone, tablet, and office computer.
